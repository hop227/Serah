Lesson 1: What is the Seerah and Why Study It?

AUTHENTIC

Explanation

The word 'Seerah' means a path or biography. Studying the Seerah means learning how Prophet Muhammad ﷺ lived his life as a leader, husband, teacher, and servant of Allah.
